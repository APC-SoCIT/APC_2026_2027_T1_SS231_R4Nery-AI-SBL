# About Us

## AI for ALL — Story-Based Learning (SBL)

*A project under the SM AI for All Initiative*

> For any mall-goer who meets AI only as a hidden feature of everyday products, AI for ALL is a story-driven learning experience that teaches basic AI concepts in minutes.

---

## What We Do

AI for ALL is a web-based learning platform that introduces basic artificial intelligence concepts to non-technical users through short, interactive stories. A mascot guides you through a branching, choice-driven story that explains ideas such as Generative AI and Machine Learning using everyday scenarios. You also try a quick hands-on AI activity, so you learn by doing and not just by reading.

- **No account needed.** Continue as a guest and finish a story right away, or register to save your progress.
- **No app to install.** Use it on a tablet at an SM caravan activity, or open it in your mobile browser.
- **Built for everyone.** The experience is designed for teens, working adults, and senior citizens, with high contrast and an accessibility-conscious design.
- **Keep learning.** When you finish a story, you get a direct link to IBM SkillsBuild to continue your AI learning journey.

## Asia Pacific College (APC)

AI for ALL is a capstone project developed by students of the **School of Computing and Information Technologies (SoCIT)** at **Asia Pacific College**, in Magallanes, Makati City. It was created for the Software Development subject, as part of the requirements for the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Software Systems specialization.

APC is the project's academic sponsor and funding source, and its faculty and adviser support the team from design through build and deployment.

---

## SM Supermalls (SM)

**SM Supermalls** is our primary client and the site host for AI for ALL. The platform is deployed during scheduled SM caravan activities, where mall-goers can walk up to a tablet and complete a short story-based lesson on their own, without staff assistance. The pilot is planned for an SM Booknook site, with later caravans following the schedule set by SM.

The system is built to run on public mall Wi-Fi and mid-range mobile devices. It does not change any of SM's internal IT systems.

---

## Our Partner

**IBM SkillsBuild** is our learning-continuation partner. After completing a story, users are offered a one-way direct link to the IBM SkillsBuild registration portal, where they can keep exploring AI at their own pace.
